      05 / About

      The Experiment.

      ME/CEE 2140 Mechanics of Materials Laboratory · Lab 3 (B) · Bending Test of Cantilevers, using the TecQuipment SM1004 Beam Apparatus.

      Objectives

      • Verify that deflection z is proportional to load W at a fixed length.
      • Verify that z is proportional to ℓ³ at a fixed load.
      • Calculate the second moment of area I from the beam dimensions.
      • Find Young’s modulus E by regression and compare it with 210 GPa (actual range 190–210 GPa).
      • Judge data quality and name the main sources of deviation.

      Material and beam

      Apparatus

      • TecQuipment SM1004 beam apparatus
      • Cantilever support (rigid clamp)
      • Load cell, 0–60 N, 10 N/mm calibration
      • Digital deflection indicator, 0–25 mm, 0.01 mm resolution
      • Mild-steel rectangular strip
      • String / tie for the opposite overhang

      Procedure

      1. Set ℓ = 200 mm, level the beam, zero the load cell and indicator.
      2. Raise the load in 2 N steps to 10 N and record z at each load.
      3. Unload slowly and record the residual zero offset.
      4. Repeat for ℓ = 300, 400, 500 and 600 mm, re-zeroing each time.
      5. Run regressions of z against W and of z against ℓ³ at 10 N.

      Sources of error

      • Beam thickness: I depends on d³, so small errors matter a lot.
      • Working length: deflection depends on ℓ³.
      • Clamp compliance or slip makes the beam seem less stiff.
      • Indicator contact force, load-cell zero and friction, and indicator alignment.
      • Residual curvature and self-weight changing the reference position.
